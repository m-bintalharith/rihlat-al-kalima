# Rihlat al-Kalima · رِحْلَةُ الْكَلِمَةِ

**A reader for advanced Arabic learners.** Classical travel accounts, speeches, stories and memoirs in Arabic, with an English translation alongside. Tap any word to learn more about it.

**Open the app:** https://m-bintalharith.github.io/rihlat-al-kalima/

## The texts

**Travel accounts · أَدَبُ الرِّحْلَاتِ**
- Ibn Fadlan’s account of the Rus (Vikings), 922 CE
- Ibn Jubayr’s account of the Normans of Sicily, 1184–85 CE
- Ibn Battuta’s Travels: Tangier, Alexandria, the Lighthouse, Cairo, 1325 CE

**Speeches and sermons · الْخُطَبُ**
- The Prophet’s ﷺ Farewell Sermon, 632 CE
- Khalid ibn al-Walid’s (RA) address at Yarmouk, 636 CE
- Tariq ibn Ziyad’s speech, 711 CE

**Stories and memoirs · الْحِكَايَاتُ وَالسِّيَرُ**
- Kalila and Dimna: three fables, c. 750 CE
- Al-Jahiz’s Book of Misers: three anecdotes, c. 850 CE
- Usama ibn Munqidh among the Franks: three episodes, c. 1183 CE

## Features

- **Tap a word:**
  - a verb shows its meaning, root, form and full conjugation table;
  - a noun shows its meaning and grammar notes;
  - a person or place shows background information.
- **Tashkeel switch:** turn the vowel marks on and off.
- **Root explorer:** see every word in the texts that shares a root.
- **My words:** save words and review them with spaced repetition.
- **Case-ending practice:** test yourself on iʿrāb.
- **Read aloud:** listen to the Arabic, whole text or sentence by sentence.
- **Reading options:** whole text or by section, continuous or by sentence, Arabic size and font, and colour by word type.
- **Timeline:** browse the texts by date.

## A note on the texts

The Arabic passages were prepared for learners. Some are shortened, and they should be checked against the standard editions before being quoted:

- Ibn Hisham for the Farewell Sermon
- al-Tabari for Yarmouk
- al-Maqqari for Tariq ibn Ziyad
- al-Dahhan for Ibn Fadlan
- Wright for Ibn Jubayr
- Defrémery–Sanguinetti for Ibn Battuta
- ʿAzzām for Kalila wa-Dimna
- al-Ḥājirī for al-Bukhalāʾ
- Hitti for Kitāb al-Iʿtibār

The English translations are original to this app.
